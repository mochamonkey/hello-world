# Child Travel Affidavit: Information Required

## Overview
When a child is traveling with only one parent, most countries and airlines require documentation to prevent child abduction and ensure legal compliance. This document outlines the essential information needed for a child travel affidavit (also known as a parental consent letter or letter of permission).

## Essential Information for the Affidavit

### 1. Traveling Parent Information
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Passport number and country of issue
- Current residential address
- Contact phone number(s)
- Email address
- Relationship to the child

### 2. Non-Traveling Parent Information
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Passport number and country of issue
- Current residential address
- Contact phone number(s)
- Email address
- Relationship to the child

### 3. Child Information
- Full legal name (as it appears on passport/ID)
- Date of birth
- Passport number and country of issue
- Place of birth
- Residential address

### 4. Travel Details
- Departure date
- Return date
- Destination country/countries
- Purpose of travel (vacation, visiting relatives, school trip, etc.)
- Flight information (if available)
- Accommodation details at destination

### 5. Consent Statement
The affidavit must include a clear statement that the non-traveling parent:
- Gives permission for the child to travel with the traveling parent
- Acknowledges the travel dates and destinations
- Confirms awareness of the trip and agrees to it

### 6. Emergency Contact Information
- Emergency contact person at home (name, phone, relationship)
- Emergency contact at destination (if applicable)
- Non-traveling parent's contact information during travel period

## Required Supporting Documents

### Must Have:
1. **Child's birth certificate** (original or certified copy)
2. **Both parents' valid identification** (passport or government-issued ID)
3. **Child's passport** (if international travel)
4. **Notarized parental consent letter** signed by non-traveling parent

### Recommended Additional Documents:
1. **Custody documents** (if applicable - court orders, custody agreements)
2. **Death certificate** (if one parent is deceased)
3. **Sole custody documentation** (if applicable)
4. **Adoption papers** (if applicable)
5. **Travel itinerary** (flight bookings, hotel reservations)
6. **Travel insurance documentation**

## Notarization Requirements

The consent letter typically must be:
- **Notarized** by a licensed notary public
- **Signed** by the non-traveling parent in the presence of the notary
- **Dated** within a reasonable timeframe of travel (check specific country requirements)
- **Original document** (not a copy) carried during travel

## Special Situations

### Single Parent
If you are a single parent with sole custody:
- Bring court documentation proving sole custody
- Bring the other parent's death certificate (if applicable)
- Consider a notarized affidavit from yourself explaining the situation

### Divorced or Separated Parents
- Custody agreement or divorce decree
- Court order granting permission to travel
- Both documents showing custody arrangements

### Child Traveling with Relatives or Friends
Additional documentation needed:
- Consent from BOTH parents
- Notarized letter authorizing the specific adult
- Copy of both parents' identification
- Proof of relationship (if applicable)

## Country-Specific Considerations

### United States
- No legal requirement for domestic travel, but airlines may request documentation
- Strongly recommended for international travel
- U.S. Customs and Border Protection may question children traveling with one parent

### Canada
- Required for children under 18 traveling internationally
- Recommended even for domestic travel
- Must be signed within a recent timeframe

### European Union
- Requirements vary by country
- Generally required for Schengen area travel
- Some countries require translation to local language

### Mexico
- Required for children under 18
- Must be notarized
- May need to be translated to Spanish

### Other Countries
- Always check destination country requirements
- Check transit country requirements
- Consult embassy or consulate for specific rules

## Sample Affidavit Format

```
PARENTAL CONSENT FOR CHILD TRAVEL

I, [Non-Traveling Parent Full Name], holder of passport number [Number], 
hereby authorize my child, [Child Full Name], born on [Date of Birth], 
holder of passport number [Number], to travel to [Destination] from 
[Departure Date] to [Return Date] with [Traveling Parent Full Name], 
holder of passport number [Number].

I acknowledge and consent to this travel arrangement.

________________________________
Signature of Non-Traveling Parent

Date: _______________

Witnessed and notarized by:
[Notary Section]
```

## Important Tips

1. **Make Multiple Copies**: Keep copies separate from originals
2. **Check Expiration Dates**: Ensure all documents are current
3. **Verify Requirements Early**: Check requirements 2-3 months before travel
4. **Contact Airlines**: Different airlines may have different policies
5. **Embassy Contact**: Verify requirements with destination country's embassy
6. **Keep Documents Accessible**: Have them readily available at all checkpoints
7. **Digital Backups**: Take photos/scans of all documents
8. **Language Translations**: Get official translations if traveling to non-English speaking countries

This document provides general information and should not be considered legal advice. Requirements vary by country, airline, and individual circumstances. Always verify current requirements with relevant authorities, airlines, and embassies before travel.
